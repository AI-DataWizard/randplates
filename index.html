Set your budget, stock your pantry — RandPlates ranks a week of dinners by real cost per plate. Search a staple below, tap it to compare retailers, then cook it.

How RandPlates prices your week

Every ingredient here carries a price — nothing is left blank. Each price is honestly tiered: ✓ checked means a person verified it against the store — on the shelf, at the till, or on the retailer's own app; ≈ estimate means it was researched from Checkers, Pick n Pay, Shoprite and comparable products, and it's marked as an estimate until someone checks it in-store. The date appears next to every price, and anything older than a month is marked old. We don't pull live feeds, so prices may shift — spot a wrong one and it gets re-verified.

You set the budget. We do the maths. Enter what's in your pantry and what you can spend this week, and RandPlates shows you what you can cook — ranked by ingredient overlap and real cost per plate. It's built as a cheap meal plan South Africa can actually use: offline, fast, no sign-up. You get seven days of meals that stay inside your budget, plus one consolidated shopping list to take to the spaza or supermarket.

What to cook with pap you already have

Your pap, rice, or morogo should lead the plan, not sit at the back. The engine checks what you already have and pushes those recipes to the top, so you use what's in your kitchen first. You'll see how much of each recipe you already cover, what's still missing, and the cost per plate in Rands and cents, lined up so you can compare. Less waste, more of your staples doing the work.

R500 grocery list or R400 — built for your budget

Whether you're planning a R500 grocery list or a tight grocery list for R400, you need numbers that match the till slip. RandPlates builds budget meals for a week South Africa can shop for — one list, total cost, priced from real local retailers. Every ingredient shows quantity and price, every meal shows cost per plate. If a plan goes over, it tells you and shows the next best fit inside your budget.

How the Decoder Lab works

What this does

Paste a messy TikTok caption, get a proper recipe draft with real rands per plate. Same caption will always give you the same draft — no AI, no guessing, just the same rules every time.

The review queue

If a line is vague — “a pinch”, a promo, a typo — it won’t guess. It moves that line to your review queue so you can fix it. Nothing dodgy auto-saves; that’s how we keep your plates honest.

Where the data lives

Everything lives on your phone. No account, no server, no one else seeing your pantry. Even offline, it still works.

What “unpriced” means

Unpriced just means we don’t have a checked shop price for that line yet. The recipe still saves, that line just counts as R0 for now. When the registry gets a real price, your plates update.

About RandPlates — built in public

RandPlates is a free, offline-first dinner planner for South African kitchens. You tell it what's in your pantry and what you can spend; it ranks what you can cook tonight and plans a priced week. No account, no server — everything stays on your phone.

How prices are researched: real retailer prices captured in-store and cross-checked online (Checkers, PnP, Shoprite) — never AI guesses. Every price carries an honesty tier: ✓ checked (verified in a real store) or ≈ estimate (researched, not yet in-store verified). It's on the card, so you always know what you're trusting.

Who builds it: I'm a final-year engineering student who saw South African kitchens scrambling for affordable meals. I used AI to stitch RandPlates together — but every price comes from a real till slip, a shelf check, the retailer's own app, or a clearly-marked estimate. Never an AI guess. No ads, no sign-up, no tracking — just your kitchen, your data.

This is a build-in-public project — the wins and the regressions get posted as they happen.
